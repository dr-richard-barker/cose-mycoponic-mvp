Phase 1: 3D Printing & Prep ~14–16 hr print • 20 min prep

  1. Slice in PETG: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layer height, 4 perimeters, 25–35% gyroid infill, no supports on lid/funnel/collar (enable Touching Buildplate support under the 8.5° drain barb only on the base).
  2. Optional Calibration (~15 min): Print mvp_fit_test_coupon.stl first to verify your printer's fit for the 90.5 mm acrylic tube, candle stem, Luer filter, 6 mm air port, and M3 holes.
  3. Clean & Dry-Fit: Clear any stringing from the 3× Ø14.2 mm wing holes, Ø13.8 mm lid pegs, and the Ø4.5 mm sloped drain bore. Test-thread the 50 mL Falcon tube into the 300° cap and the candle stem into the bottom of the double-funnel.

Phase 2: Mechanical & Chamber Assembly ~20 min

  1. Mount Ceramic Candle: Slide the rubber washer onto the 1/4" BSP stem, pass it through mvp_candle_collar.stl, and tighten the wing nut. Wrap 2–3 turns of PTFE tape on the upper threads.
  2. Assemble Lid & Double-Funnel: Drop the candle collar into the center pocket of mvp_top_lid_pegs.stl. Align the 3× Ø14.2 mm wing holes of mvp_syringe_cage_snap_falcon_funnel.stl onto the 3× upward lid pegs and screw the funnel's bottom 1/4" BSP boss onto the candle stem until flush.
  3. Install Filter, Feed Syringe & Falcon Bubbler: Press the 0.22 μm Luer-slip filter into the 0° lid port. Seat the 50 mL nutrient syringe into the top cage + Luer-Lock socket. Fill a 50 mL Falcon tube ~1/3 with sterile water and screw it upward into the 300° cap.
  4. Seat Tube, Lid & Collection Syringe: Press the 85×90×100 mm acrylic tube into mvp_bottom_base_collection.stl, place the lid assembly on top, and snap the 10 mL collection syringe into the 75° C-clips.

Phase 3: Plumbing & Fluidics ~10 min

  1. Drain Line (0° → 75°): Push 1/4" ID silicone tubing the full 16 mm depth onto the 8.5° sloped drain barb, insert the 1/4" inline ball valve mid-line, and route to the 10 mL collection syringe. Keep the valve closed during runs and open to sample leachate.
  2. Air Humidifier Loop (285° → 300° → 240°): Snap the 5V pump into the 285° clips. Run 6 mm OD air tubing from pump outlet → 300° Falcon cap Port A (with dip tube submerged in water), and from Port B (headspace) → 240° lid air port.

SHT30 Probe:  VCC (Red) → 3.3V | GND (Black) → GND | SDA → GPIO 21 | SCL → GPIO 22
Critical Polarity & Voltage Checks: (1) The 1N4007 silver cathode band MUST face +5V. (2) Power the SHT30 probe from ESP32 3.3V (not 5V). (3) Never brush conformal coating onto the SHT30 sintered tip, USB port, or BOOT/EN buttons.
  1. Solder MOSFET Driver: With the IRLZ44N face toward you (legs down), Pin 1 = Gate, Pin 2 = Drain, Pin 3 = Source. Solder 10 kΩ between Gate & Source, 220 Ω from GPIO 25 to Gate, and the 1N4007 diode across Pump +/-. Wire PSU +5V/GND via the screw terminal block to ESP32 VIN/GND and the pump/MOSFET.
  2. Route SHT30 Probe: Hang the SHT30 tip through the 120° lid port into the chamber airspace (clear of the wet candle) and wire to 3.3V, GND, GPIO 21, GPIO 22.
  3. Coat & Mount: Brush MG Chemicals 422B over exposed solder joints and PCB traces (~30 min tack-free, 24 hr full cure). Mount the ESP32 in the 180° base bay with M3×8 mm stainless screws.

Phase 5: Software Installation & Flashing ~20–30 min

  1. Install Arduino IDE 2.x (~5 min): Download from arduino.cc/en/software.
  2. Install ESP32 Board Core (~10 min): Open Preferences, add https://raw.githubusercontent.com/espressif/arduino-esp32/gh-pages/package_esp32_index.json to Additional boards manager URLs, then open Boards Manager and install esp32 by Espressif Systems.
  3. Install SHT31 Library (~2 min): Open Library Manager, search Adafruit SHT31, and install (including Adafruit BusIO).
  4. USB Driver (~3 min, if needed): Plug ESP32 via a data Micro-USB cable. If no port appears under Tools → Port, install the Silicon Labs CP210x (or CH340) VCP driver.
  5. Upload Firmware (~5 min): Open firmware/esp32_humid_controller/esp32_humid_controller.ino, select Board: DOIT ESP32 DEVKIT V1 and your COM/cu port, then click Upload (hold BOOT if it waits on Connecting...).
  6. Verify Serial Monitor: Set baud rate to 115200; confirm readings like T=22.40 C | RH=64.2% | Pump=ON every 2 seconds.

Phase 6: Leak Test & Commissioning ~15 min

  1. Hysteresis Test: Power via the 5V 2A PSU. Confirm the pump runs below 85% RH and shuts off above 90% RH (or after the 120 s safety cap).
  2. Bubbler & Drain Check: Verify steady bubbling in the 50 mL Falcon tube with no air leaks, close the 1/4" drain valve, and load sterile water/nutrient into the top 50 mL syringe to confirm slow ceramic candle weeping.
