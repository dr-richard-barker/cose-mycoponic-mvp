CoSE Mycoponic Chamber — MVP

A pragmatic, 3D-printable single-chamber bioreactor built around an off-the-shelf 85 × 90 × 100 mm clear acrylic tube and a 4-inch ceramic filter candle, with Luer syringe-filter gas exchange, humid air & sensor ports, and a funneling drain base.

Interactive Orthographic 3D Assembly (with 50 mL & 10 mL Syringes)

How It Functions

1. Nutrient Feed (Top)

50 mL Syringe → Ceramic Candle

A 50 mL (or 25 mL) syringe feeds liquid nutrients into the hollow stem of the 4-inch ceramic candle, weeping radially outward through the porous wall.

2. Controlled Headspace

Humid Air, Sensor & Syringe Filter

An external pump pushes humidified air into the 6.0 mm inlet, a probe hangs through the 8.0 mm port, and exhaust vents through a 0.22 μm Luer syringe filter.

3. Gravity Sump

38.5° Conical Funnel & Drain

Excess exudate/nutrients drip off the candle into the 38.5° funnel and exit horizontally through a 1/4" drain barb (20 mm off the bench) to an inline valve.

4. Electronics & Collection

Arched Bays & 10 mL Syringe Cradle

Three smooth arched bays under the funnel house Arduinos/sensors (M3 holes), while twin C-clips hold a 10 mL collection syringe flush at the base edge.
